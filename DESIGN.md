# PlanTravel interface

The product is a Vietnamese travel planning tool. Its task screens prioritize readable itineraries, maps, expenses and reliable actions. The dashboard adds photographic inspiration without inventing trip data.

- Preserve the existing Source Sans Pro typeface, deep pine `#14342B`, white panels and burnt orange action color `#B94E28`. Use the quiet green canvas `#F3F6F3` and borders `#DDE5DE`.
- Use orange for primary actions, green for navigation and selected states. Keep secondary actions neutral. Avoid decorative gradients; a scrim over destination photography is appropriate for readable text.
- Dashboard: a split photographic opening, compact real trip statistics and destination photographs. Empty and populated states use the same hierarchy.
- Trip setup: contextual introduction and photograph next to the form on desktop; a single column on smaller screens. Maps keep controls outside the canvas.
- The destination field offers six immediate choices and filters them with the existing accent-insensitive destination catalog as the user types. Use ordinary, keyboard-accessible buttons that fill the field without submitting; allow free text for other destinations. Do not show a second native suggestion popup on this setup form.
- Use existing local destination photographs and preserve their image credits. Inspiration photography must be labeled as a destination, never as a user's saved trip.
- Motion: one bounded photo reveal on the dashboard; fast feedback for controls; 180ms disclosure transitions. Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`, keep content usable without animation, and avoid continuous decorative effects.
- Maintain visible keyboard focus, readable contrast, native form affordances and no horizontal page overflow at 390px.
- Public landing page: the user requested pronounced effects in both scroll directions. Use a held Vietnam journey with scroll-driven route drawing and destination photos, opposing hero-title movement, a moving geographic title and an opening landscape at the close. Keep native scrolling, controls readable, and use normal document flow for reduced motion and short phone screens. Task screens retain the restrained motion above.
- Smooth scroll effects through bounded springs; use transforms for the closing frame and photo reveals. Keep native scrolling and avoid repeated React state updates on every scroll frame. Serve a lighter 720p video to phones; reduced motion loads no video source.
- AI and manual planner share the same introductory spread, destination photo and form hierarchy. The user removed the introductory AI/manual switch; keep mode navigation in the sidebar. Continue forest selection states, flat surfaces and readable type through the destination picker and itinerary editor.
- Community stories at `/community` are public to read. Publishing, commenting and rating require sign-in. Only the author edits/deletes their story; one editable rating per reader, with no self-rating. Empty accounts show real empty states, never sample stories or invented counts.
- A community post shares a saved plan, with its name, destination, dates, planned budget, transport and daily stops including times, addresses and map links. Experience text is optional. Publish a snapshot so later private edits do not silently change the experienced itinerary; keep members, actual expenses, messages and private notes out of it.
- Show the complete plan directly in the feed and the sharing preview, without a collapsed disclosure. Render each scheduled day, including an explicit empty state for days without saved stops. Community dialogs hide the visible scrollbar while preserving wheel, touch and keyboard scrolling.
- Allow up to eight photos from files, with previews/removal and the first photo as cover. Resize camera images before submitting; validate file contents, size and dimensions on the server. Store public images under `backend/uploads/community/`; remove discarded photos and deleted-post photos. Label fallback destination photography as illustration.
- The landing page never mounts the chat widget, including signed-in sessions. Remove notifications from sidebar navigation; retain the existing navbar notification entry.
